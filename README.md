# Tend — A Better Way to Access Nature

Tend is a digital access platform designed to make it easier, safer, and more transparent for people to enjoy private land while giving landholders confidence, control, and fair compensation. It connects walkers, riders, anglers, birders, and families with the landscapes they love — and gives landholders a simple, low‑friction way to manage access on their terms.



### Why Tend Exists

Australia has extraordinary natural assets, but access is fragmented, informal, and often risky for both visitors and landholders. Traditional access arrangements rely on trust, local knowledge, and manual processes that don’t scale.



Tend solves this by creating a trusted digital layer between people and place.



### What Tend Does

**Digital Permissions** — Visitors request access through the app; landholders approve with one tap.



**Clear Rules \& Expectations** — Every visit is governed by a simple, standardised access agreement.



**Safety \& Accountability** — Identity verification, check‑ins, and clear boundaries reduce risk.



**Landholder Control** — Set availability, conditions, pricing, and limits.



**Visitor Experience** — Discover new places, understand what’s allowed, and enjoy nature with confidence.



### The Value Proposition

#### For Landholders

* Reduce risk and uncertainty
* Earn income from low‑impact access
* Maintain control over who enters and when
* Replace ad‑hoc arrangements with a simple, auditable system

#### For Visitors

* Find unique places to explore
* Know the rules before you arrive
* Book and pay in seconds
* Support local land stewards

#### The Market Opportunity

* Outdoor recreation is growing rapidly, and demand for nature‑based experiences is outpacing supply. Private land represents a vast, underutilised asset class. Tend unlocks this value by creating a scalable, trusted marketplace for access.

#### Our Vision

* A world where connecting with nature is easy, safe, and mutually beneficial — where landholders are empowered stewards, visitors are responsible participants, and landscapes thrive.
