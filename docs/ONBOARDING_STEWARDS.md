# Steward Onboarding Guide

This document explains the process for onboarding new stewards to the Tend platform, including verification, permissions, and operational responsibilities.

---

## 1. Who Are Stewards?

Stewards are trusted individuals who manage access to land parcels on behalf of landholders. They ensure safe, respectful, and compliant visitor experiences.

---

## 2. Required Information

Stewards must provide:

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Proof of identity
- Landholder association (property, organisation)
- Bank details (for payouts)
- Emergency contact details

---

## 3. Verification Process

1. Identity verification
2. Landholder approval
3. Property association confirmation
4. Stripe onboarding for payouts
5. Steward account activation

---

## 4. Steward Permissions

Stewards can:

- Approve or decline access requests
- Set availability windows
- Add safety notes
- Manage property boundaries
- View visitor logs
- Trigger emergency alerts

---

## 5. Steward Responsibilities

- Ensure visitor safety
- Maintain accurate availability
- Respond to access requests promptly
- Report incidents
- Maintain property compliance

---

## 6. Support & Escalation

- Level 1: Steward support team
- Level 2: Landholder liaison
- Level 3: Platform admin
